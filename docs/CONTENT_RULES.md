# Content Rules

## Required for Commercial Articles

- Clear opening verdict
- Who the article is for
- Comparison table
- Pricing section
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Best-for use cases
- Alternatives
- FAQ
- Visible affiliate disclosure
- Last verified date

## Prohibited

- Fake reviews
- Fake screenshots
- Fake user counts
- Fake pricing
- Fake commission data
- Fake first-hand experience
- Fake affiliate links or invented tracking IDs
- Vendor copy pasted into articles
- Thin article variants created only for scale

## Verification

Use official product, pricing, help, and affiliate pages when possible. If a fact is unclear, mark it `VERIFY_BEFORE_PUBLISH` and avoid presenting it as confirmed.

Future review/comparison articles synthesize official facts + verified public review consensus + recent community feedback, without pretending to have used products when no actual hands-on test occurred.

For the complete operating model, see `docs/EDITORIAL_PLAYBOOK.md`.
