# Research Snapshot — 2026-08-22

## Kit
- Official pricing: https://kit.com/pricing
- Free Newsletter plan: up to 10,000 subscribers.
- Creator: $33/mo for 1,000 subscribers example.
- Pro: $66/mo for 1,000 subscribers example.
- G2: 4.4/5, 236 reviews.
- Capterra: 4.6/5, 243 reviews; Ease 4.4; Customer Service 4.4.
- Recurring praise: ease of use, segmentation, visual automations, integrations/forms.
- Recurring criticism: pricing as lists grow; template/design control for some users.

## beehiiv
- Official pricing: https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing
- Launch: $0, up to 2,500 subscribers.
- Scale: $43/mo billed annually.
- Max: $96/mo billed annually.
- G2: 4.5/5, 40 reviews.
- Recurring praise: monetization, ad network, publishing/design, sharing/recommendations.
- Community caution: some recent reports of editor/migration friction; treat as anecdotal.

## ActiveCampaign
- G2: 4.4/5, ~14,677 reviews.
- Capterra: 4.6/5, 2,568 reviews; Ease 4.2; Customer Service 4.4.
- Official pricing page verified plan names: Starter, Plus, Pro, Enterprise. Public price display depends on contact count, currency, and configuration selectors; treat specific price examples as snapshots requiring live confirmation.
- Recurring praise: automation, segmentation, integrations, CRM/customer journeys.
- Recurring criticism: learning curve, price, some email-builder/reporting friction.

## MailerLite
- G2: 4.6/5, 1,100+ reviews.
- Capterra: 4.7/5, 2,322 reviews; Ease 4.6; Customer Service 4.8.
- Recurring praise: easy UI, affordability, forms/landing pages, campaign building, support.
- Recurring criticism: less advanced CRM/automation/reporting; occasional verification/compliance/support complaints.

## Flodesk
- Capterra: 4.5/5, 167 reviews; Ease 4.7.
- Recurring praise: design/templates, ease, checkout/landing-page simplicity, help resources.
- Watch: compare automation/reporting depth for complex operations.

## Substack
- Official pricing/help: publishing free; 10% fee on paid subscription transactions plus payment processing.
- Community themes are mixed: some value Notes/discovery; others report attention/discovery friction.

## Ghost
- Official pricing: Starter $18/mo billed yearly; Publisher $29/mo billed yearly.
- Publisher adds paid subscriptions/custom themes/advanced analytics.
- Ghost says it takes no platform payment fee; payment processing still applies.

## Evidence rule
Official facts > verified marketplace consensus > community anecdotes.
